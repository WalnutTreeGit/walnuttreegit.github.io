Hi! I'm João Paulo. I hold an MSc in **Computer Science** (Networks) from **Institut Polytechnique de Paris**, awarded with highest honors, and a BSc in **Electrical and Computer Engineering** from the **Polytechnic Institute of Porto**.

Most recently, I was a **Machine Learning Research Intern** at **Nokia Bell Labs** (Paris), where I proposed "Certainty-Guided Reasoning", a framework for dynamic thinking budgets in LLMs. This work received the **Outstanding Innovation Award** at the Nokia Bell Labs Global Student Program (2025). I also co-authored a **NeurIPS 2026** paper on structured causal reasoning in LLMs. Before that, I was a Robotics Research Intern at **INESC TEC**, building LiDAR drivers and mapping tools for agricultural field robots. In July 2026 I attended the **Imperial College Robotics Summer School**, where I trained RL locomotion policies that transferred zero-shot to a Unitree Go2.

### Research Interests

* **Robotics & Autonomy:** VLA models, Embodied Intelligence, Long-Term Autonomy, Semantic Failure Recovery.
* **Formal Methods:** SMT-based Constraint Learning, Adaptive Safety Shields.
* **Machine Learning:** Inference-time reasoning, Uncertainty quantification, Efficient LLMs.
