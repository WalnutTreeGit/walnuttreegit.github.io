Our paper "**Externalized CPDAG Summaries Improve LLM Causal Deduction**" has been accepted at **NeurIPS 2026** as a poster. We show that asking an LLM to first write out a structured causal summary (a CPDAG), and then answer against it, substantially improves causal reasoning: on the Corr2Cause benchmark it raises F1 by 13.4 points. [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31071)
