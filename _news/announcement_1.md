I am honored to receive the **Outstanding Innovation Award** from the Nokia Bell Labs Global Student Program 2025. Out of a global pool of interns, I was selected as a finalist in the AI category and became the first France-based intern to receive this distinction. This award recognizes my research on "Certainty-Guided Reasoning," a framework for dynamic thinking budgets in LLMs.
